Edits save automatically on this device only. To show your version to someone else, tap Share and copy the view-only link. They just open it. No file needed.

How to use this map

Find something fast. Type an aisle or product in the search box and tap a result. The map zooms to it and flashes it.

Look around. Drag to scroll, pinch or use the +/− buttons to zoom, double-tap empty space to zoom in or back out, and tap Fit to see the whole store.

Edit an item. In Edit mode, tap a box and a small bar appears right above it: Rename, Color, Rotate, Copy, Delete. Drag the corner square to resize it, or drag the selected box to move it. More settings are in the panel.

Move lots of things. Switch to Move mode to drag any item directly. Use two fingers to scroll and zoom while in Move mode.

Print or PDF. Print / PDF lets you pick the paper size and shows a preview first. Choose Save as PDF as the printer to make a PDF.

Share. Changes save automatically on this device. Share gives you a view-only link for your boss and coworkers, a picture, or a backup file.
